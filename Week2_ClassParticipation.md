# MIT653-Research-Methods-Group-2
This is my MIT653 Research Methods group project repository for "AI in education &amp; elearning platforms" where I have developed a research planning &amp; conducting research into AI in Education and E-Learning Platforms that assesses impact of personalized learning through AI powered adaptive learning algorithm enabled Elearning platform on students.

# Week 2 - Class Participation

## Research Question
How effective are AI-powered e-learning platforms in delivering personalised learning experiences through adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery for university students?

## Hypothesis
AI-powered e-learning platforms that use adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery significantly improve students' learning experiences, engagement, and academic performance compared to traditional e-learning platforms.

## Evaluation

### Research Question: Good

Reason:
- Focused and specific.
- Relevant to AI in Education.
- Feasible to investigate.
- Significant because it contributes to improving personalised learning.

### Hypothesis: Good

Reason:
- Specific and measurable.
- Testable using empirical research.
- Directly addresses the research question.
