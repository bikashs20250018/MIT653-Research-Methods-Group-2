The literature review process describes how relevant academic literature was identified, selected and analysed.

### Literature Sources

The group focused primarily on:

- Scoping reviews.

The selected databases/search platforms included:

The main search keywords included:

- Artificial Intelligence.
- AI in Education.

- Adaptive learning algorithms.
- Machine learning.
- E-learning.

- Tailored content.
- Adaptive content delivery.
- Learning outcomes.

### Search Strategy

Keywords were combined using Boolean operators such as **AND** and **OR**.

Example search:

> `"Artificial Intelligence" AND "e-learning" AND "personalized learning" AND "adaptive learning"`

Additional searches combined terms relating to AI, personalised learning, adaptive learning, machine learning, intelligent tutoring systems and learning outcomes.

### Inclusion Criteria

Studies were considered relevant when they:

- focused on AI, machine learning or intelligent tutoring in education;
- discussed personalised or adaptive learning;
- related to e-learning, online learning or digital learning;
- examined adaptive algorithms or personalised content delivery;
- investigated learning outcomes, engagement or academic performance;
- were relevant to the research question;
- were academic publications;
- were available in English.

### Exclusion Criteria

The group excluded:

- irrelevant studies;
- duplicate publications;
- non-academic websites and blogs;
- opinion-based sources without research evidence;
- studies unrelated to education;
- studies unrelated to personalised or adaptive learning;
- sources that did not contribute to answering the research question.

# 4. Literature Analysis

The selected literature was reviewed and compared to identify common findings, methodologies, themes, research gaps and unresolved issues.

The analysis focused on how AI-powered e-learning platforms provide personalised learning and how their effectiveness can be evaluated.

---

## 4.1 AI in Personalised Education

The literature indicates that Artificial Intelligence can support personalised education by analysing learner information, performance and behaviour.

AI-based systems can use learner data to provide personalised recommendations, learning pathways, feedback and educational resources.

The literature also identifies intelligent tutoring systems as an important application of AI in education.

---

## 4.2 Adaptive Learning Algorithms

Adaptive learning systems can adjust learning experiences according to individual learner needs and performance.

The literature identifies the use of:

- Machine learning.
- Learner modelling.
- Learner profiling.
- Adaptive assessment.
- Personalised recommendations.
- Dynamic learning pathways.

These approaches allow learning platforms to provide different content or learning activities depending on the learner's progress and requirements.

---

## 4.3 Tailored Content Delivery

AI-powered e-learning platforms can personalise content by considering factors such as:

- Learner performance.
- Previous knowledge.
- Learning progress.
- Learning preferences.
- Individual learning needs.

Tailored content delivery can allow learners to receive appropriate learning materials and feedback instead of following exactly the same learning pathway as every other student.

---

# 5. Common Themes Identified

The group identified the following common themes across the literature:

### Theme 1 – Personalised Learning

AI can support learning experiences that are adapted to individual learner needs.

### Theme 2 – Adaptive Learning

Adaptive systems can modify learning content and pathways according to learner performance.

### Theme 3 – Intelligent Tutoring Systems

Intelligent tutoring systems can provide personalised instruction, feedback and learning support.

### Theme 4 – Learning Outcomes

Several studies investigate whether AI-supported personalised learning can improve academic performance and learning outcomes.

### Theme 5 – Student Engagement

Personalised and adaptive learning can support student participation, motivation and engagement.

### Theme 6 – Implementation Challenges

The literature also identifies challenges involving privacy, ethics, algorithmic bias, implementation and the role of educators.

---

# 6. Research Gaps and Unresolved Issues

The literature review identified several areas requiring further research.

## 6.1 Long-Term Effectiveness

More research is required to determine whether improvements associated with AI-powered personalised learning continue over longer periods.

## 6.2 Comparison of Adaptive Algorithms

Different adaptive learning and machine-learning approaches require further comparison to determine which approaches are most effective in different educational contexts.

## 6.3 Measurement of Effectiveness

Different studies use different measures of effectiveness, including academic performance, engagement, satisfaction and learning outcomes. More consistent evaluation approaches could improve comparison between studies.

## 6.4 Privacy and Ethical Issues

AI-powered personalisation requires learner data, creating concerns about privacy, responsible data use and ethical implementation.

## 6.5 Algorithmic Bias

AI systems may produce biased recommendations if the data or algorithms used to train them contain bias.

## 6.6 Teacher Involvement

Further research is required to understand how educators can effectively work with AI-powered personalised learning systems.

## 6.7 Educational Context

The effectiveness of AI-powered personalised learning may differ depending on the institution, subject, learners and learning environment.

---

# 7. Contribution to the Research Question

The reviewed literature provides evidence that AI-powered e-learning platforms can support personalised education through adaptive learning algorithms, intelligent tutoring systems and tailored content delivery.

The literature also indicates that effectiveness should be evaluated using measurable outcomes such as:

- Academic performance.
- Learning outcomes.
- Student engagement.
- Learner satisfaction.
- Learning progress.

However, the literature demonstrates that effectiveness is influenced by factors such as system design, algorithm selection, implementation, learner characteristics, educational context and teacher involvement.

Therefore, the literature provides a foundation for answering our research question:

> **How effective are AI-powered e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education through adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery?**
