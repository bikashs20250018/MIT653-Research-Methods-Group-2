# Task 4 – Literature Review Structure

## Research Topic

**AI in Education or E-Learning Platforms for Personalized Education: Analyse the effectiveness of e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education experiences, considering adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery.**

## Research Question

**How effective are AI-powered e-learning platforms in delivering personalised education through adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery?**

---

## Proposed Literature Review Structure

### 1. Introduction and Background

- Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI) in education.
- Development and importance of e-learning platforms.
- Concept of personalised education.
- Role of AI in delivering personalised learning experiences.
- Importance of adaptive learning algorithms and tailored content delivery.
- Research problem and research question.

---

### 2. Purpose and Aims of the Literature Review

- Explain the purpose of reviewing existing research.
- Examine the use of AI in personalised education.
- Investigate adaptive learning algorithms in e-learning platforms.
- Examine tailored content delivery.
- Analyse effects on student learning outcomes and engagement.
- Identify research gaps and unresolved issues.
- Establish how existing research contributes to the research question.

---

### 3. Literature Review Process

#### 3.1 Literature Sources

- Peer-reviewed journal articles
- Empirical research studies
- Systematic literature reviews
- Scoping reviews
- Meta-analyses

#### 3.2 Academic Databases

- Scopus
- IEEE Xplore
- ScienceDirect
- ERIC
- Google Scholar

#### 3.3 Search Keywords

- Artificial Intelligence
- AI in Education
- AI-powered e-learning
- Personalised Learning
- Personalised Education
- Adaptive Learning
- Adaptive Learning Algorithms
- Machine Learning
- E-Learning
- Intelligent Tutoring Systems
- Tailored Content
- Adaptive Content Delivery
- Learning Outcomes
- Student Engagement
- Academic Performance

#### 3.4 Search Strategy

Boolean operators such as **AND** and **OR** will be used to combine keywords.

Example:

> `"Artificial Intelligence" AND "e-learning" AND "personalized learning" AND "adaptive learning"`

#### 3.5 Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

The literature selection criteria will be explained, including:

- Relevance to AI and education.
- Relevance to personalised or adaptive learning.
- Relevance to e-learning platforms.
- Peer-reviewed academic sources.
- English-language publications.
- Exclusion of duplicate, irrelevant and non-academic sources.

---

### 4. AI in Personalised Education

This section will examine:

- Applications of AI in education.
- AI-powered e-learning platforms.
- Intelligent tutoring systems.
- Machine learning in education.
- Learner modelling.
- Personalised learning pathways.

The selected literature will be compared to understand how AI supports individual learner needs.

---

### 5. Adaptive Learning Algorithms and Tailored Content Delivery

This section will focus on:

- Adaptive learning systems.
- Machine learning algorithms.
- Learner performance analysis.
- Learner profiling.
- Dynamic learning pathways.
- Adaptive assessment.
- Personalised recommendations.
- Tailored educational content.

The literature will be analysed to understand how adaptive technologies modify learning experiences according to individual learner characteristics and performance.

---

### 6. Effectiveness of AI-Powered E-Learning Platforms

This section will analyse the effectiveness of personalised e-learning using:

#### 6.1 Academic Performance

- Learning outcomes
- Test scores
- Knowledge acquisition
- Academic achievement

#### 6.2 Student Engagement

- Participation
- Motivation
- Interaction
- Course completion

#### 6.3 Personalised Learning Experience

- Individual learning pathways
- Personalised feedback
- Appropriate content difficulty
- Learner satisfaction

The findings and methodologies of the selected research papers will be compared and synthesised.

---

### 7. Common Themes Across the Literature

The literature will be synthesised around the following themes:

1. Personalised learning.
2. Adaptive learning.
3. Intelligent tutoring systems.
4. Machine learning and learner modelling.
5. Tailored content delivery.
6. Academic performance.
7. Student engagement.
8. AI implementation challenges.

---

### 8. Research Gaps and Unresolved Issues

This section will identify gaps emerging from the selected literature, including:

- Limited long-term evaluation of AI-powered personalised learning.
- Limited comparison between adaptive learning algorithms.
- Lack of standardised measures for evaluating effectiveness.
- Privacy and ethical concerns related to learner data.
- Algorithmic bias.
- Limited research on teacher involvement.
- Differences across educational contexts.
- Need for further empirical comparisons of AI-powered e-learning platforms.

---

### 9. Conclusion

The conclusion will:

- Summarise the key findings from the literature.
- Explain the effectiveness of AI-powered personalised learning.
- Highlight the role of adaptive learning algorithms.
- Summarise the benefits of tailored content delivery.
- Restate the major research gaps.
- Relate the findings back to the research question.
- Identify potential directions for future research.

---

### 10. References

The final literature review will include the academic references used throughout the review.

The references will include the **10 selected research articles** identified and analysed by the group during the Week 4 literature search.
